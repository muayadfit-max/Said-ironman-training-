    His week

      Coach notes

      Send this with the plan

      Warm up — 5 minutes, every session

      • 1 min marching or walking on the spot
      • 10 arm circles each way, 10 shoulder rolls
      • 10 slow bodyweight squats — movement only, no load
      • 10 hip swings each leg, then 20s easy jog on the spot
      • Run days: add 1 minute of easy jogging before the clock starts

      When to stop

      • Stop the set the moment form breaks — hips sagging, back arching, neck straining. A clean 8 beats a messy 15.
      • Stop the session for chest pain, dizziness, sharp joint pain, or breath you can't catch. Message me the same day.
      • Sore for a day or two is normal. Sore past 3 days means we did too much — tell me and we cut the next session back.

      About the numbers

      • The VO2 max figure is an estimate from a running test, not a lab result, and it's calculated on the low side on purpose. It's a starting line to beat, not a grade.
      • Bodyweight scores are compared to his own last test — nobody else's.
      • The all-out 9-minute run happens once. Every other run is talking pace: if he can't say a full sentence, he slows down.
      • Re-test everything at the end of week 4, not sooner.
      Method. VO2 max uses the Cooper 12-minute run formula, (metres − 504.9) ÷ 44.73, applied to the 9-minute distance scaled up by ×1.28 rather than the naive ×1.33 (pace always fades over the extra 3 minutes), then 1.0 ml/kg/min is subtracted as a youth pacing margin. Categories follow the FITNESSGRAM Healthy Fitness Zone for 16-year-old boys. Working sets are set at 50–60% of near-failure test reps, so no session is trained to failure. Estimates for coaching, not medical screening.
